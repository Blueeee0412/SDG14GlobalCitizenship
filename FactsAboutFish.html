Anemone image

Anemone live with clownfish, they give them a home, and the clownfish clean them of parasites. They can extend out tentacles to catch prey. Are considered animals. Are venomous but clownfish have adapted to be able to live close to them, being immune to the poison ​

Goblin sharks have jaws can detach from their face and extend to catch prey. The jaw is not attached to the skull. Survived through the dinosaur extinction. Can sense electrical fields in the dark ​
