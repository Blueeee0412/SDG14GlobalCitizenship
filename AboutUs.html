We are a group of students working on a project focused on SDG 14: Life Below Water. Our aim is to raise awareness about the problems affecting our oceans and marine life, including pollution, overfishing and climate change. Through this website, we want to share information about these issues and highlight the importance of protecting our oceans for future generations

As part of our project, we have also developed an idea for an underwater monitoring device that could help detect changes in water quality and help protect our oceans. We believe that protecting our oceans starts with understanding them! !
