Fish populations in many parts of the world are under pressure from human activities and environmental changes.One of the biggest causes is overfishing, this occurs when fish are caught faster than they can reproduce, making it difficult for populations to recover. According to the Food and Agriculture Organisation (FAO, 2025), 35.5% of the marine fish stocks studied are overfished. ​

Fish and other marine animals can mistake plastic for food or become trapped in waste. Pollution from farms and wastewater can also cause algal blooms, which uses up oxygen in the water
